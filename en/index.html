Fluidity & Technique

Hidden lightness within rigid metal

Emanuel Nunes' work challenges the very nature of his mediums. His creations cut, bend, and texture noble metals to extract dynamism and organic lines that appear to float seamlessly within urban architectures or private master collections.

Premium Finishing

The perfection of absolute reflection

Far beyond pure volumetric form, his signature lies in an obsession with high-end polishing and advanced chemical surface treatments, such as custom Nickel plating and surgical handling of Stainless Steel. Light and reflections become an active part of the sculptural experience.
